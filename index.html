About

I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science and Engineering at The Chinese University of Hong Kong, advised by Prof. Bei Yu. My research has two closely connected directions: LLM4EDA / AI4EDA and 3D IC design automation.

On the AI side, I study multi-modal circuit representations, cross-stage knowledge distillation, and LLM-based verification and optimization. On the 3D IC side, I develop legality-aware RTL-to-GDS flows and early-stage timing models for hybrid-bonded designs. My current interests include tool-using agents for chip implementation and the interaction of data paths, bonding parasitics, and clock networks in setup/hold timing closure.

Before joining CUHK, I received my master's degree in Electronic Information from the University of Chinese Academy of Sciences and the Institute of Computing Technology, Chinese Academy of Sciences, advised by Prof. Xiaowei Li and Prof. Huawei Li at the State Key Laboratory of Processors. I received my bachelor's degree in Microelectronics Science and Technology from Peking University, and have industrial EDA experience with CASTEST on fault simulation and ISO 26262-oriented functional safety analysis.
